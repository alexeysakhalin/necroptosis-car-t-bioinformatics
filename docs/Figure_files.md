Use the PDF files for final layout when supported by the journal. Keep the aspect ratio when placing a panel. The 41-gene heatmap is designed at approximately 183 mm width. Multi-panel UMAP and GSEA figures need full text width. Enrichment panels are best placed as full-page figures; the PDF permits inspection of long term labels without raster enlargement. Panel letters for insertion can be added in the manuscript layout; 3D and 3E already carry their letters.

These files replace the indicated bioinformatics panels. They are not composites containing the original microscopy, immunoblot or other experimental panels. Figure 6 is not included.
