# Data sources and reuse

The source manifest records the exact files used in the analysis, their byte sizes and SHA-256 checksums. Source measurements are kept separate from derived tables. Third-party data retain their original terms and are not relicensed by the software license.

Localized spreadsheet interface, style, theme and print-footer labels were standardized to English, and local save-directory metadata were removed from the supplied workbooks. Cell data, worksheet names and molecular counts were unchanged. Original-archive hashes, distributed-file hashes and worksheet cell-data hashes are all recorded in the manifest.

| --- | --- | --- |

| Study targeted single-cell expression | Four supplied Supplementary Tables S1–S4, using the CD3-positive worksheets | Study authors; four samples, 259 genes, 11,132 analyzed cells |

The full DepMap source matrix and Model.csv are not included in the public distribution. Both supplied files match **DepMap Public 25Q3**, released **25 September 2025**, in the official download catalogue. Download that release from https://depmap.org/portal/download/all/ and place the files at the paths in the manifest. Do not substitute the latest release. `config/depmap_release.json` records the release, catalogue MD5 values and independently calculated SHA-256 values. The downstream selected models, panel values and scores are included. DepMap data remain subject to the portal terms at https://depmap.org/portal/terms/; the software license does not apply to these source data.

The CTRPv2 results use the data released by the Broad Institute through the NCI CTD² community resource. They are based in part on data generated by the Cancer Target Discovery and Development Network established by the National Cancer Institute. Cite Basu et al. 2013, Seashore-Ludlow et al. 2015 and Rees et al. 2016; their verified references are supplied. The original resource and CTD² data-use guidance remain applicable: https://www.cancer.gov/ccg/research/functional-genomics/ctd2/using-ctd2-data.

The selected MSigDB gene sets and GO annotations are provided with their source attribution. MSigDB's terms are at https://www.gsea-msigdb.org/gsea/msigdb/license.jsp. GO data are distributed under CC BY 4.0; the Bioconductor annotation packages also record their package licenses in `environment/R_packages.tsv`.

The complete KEGG association database is excluded from the distribution. The pipeline retrieves it from the official service for use under KEGG's applicable terms. Derived enrichment statistics, query overlaps, resource hashes and release information are supplied. No KEGG pathway-map images are reproduced.

Study sequence reads are registered under accession https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/PRJNA1452641. Reviewer-only access links are not part of the analysis package. The source data used here are processed differential-expression summaries and targeted expression matrices; FASTQ processing and bulk count-model fitting are outside this downstream release.

Exact reproduction of KEGG annotation membership requires the recorded 4 October 2026 resources. The analysis refuses a different snapshot unless a new analysis is explicitly requested. The distributed contingency counts and complete tested-term tables permit independent verification of every reported hypergeometric probability and Benjamini–Hochberg adjustment without redistributing the KEGG database; use `python scripts/verify_cached_results.py`. This verifies the saved statistics, not the original annotation membership.

The inspected accession records were scheduled for release on 29 December 2026. Public read access must be confirmed when the manuscript is published; a registered accession does not itself establish current public availability.
