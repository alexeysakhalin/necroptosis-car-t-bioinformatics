# Bioinformatics results

Applying the stated expression and significance filters to the supplied differential-expression summaries identified 227 upregulated and 35 downregulated genes in the T-cell comparison and 594 upregulated and 402 downregulated genes in the TNF+IFNγ comparison. Ninety-three upregulated genes were shared, with 134 and 501 additional genes meeting the criteria only in the T-cell and cytokine comparisons, respectively. The shared response included CXCL9, CXCL10, IDO1, CD74, GBP-family members, CXCL11, PARP14, CTSS, TRIM22, XAF1 and AIM2 (Fig. 4A–C). These threshold-defined sets describe overlap between the two comparisons; they do not directly test a treatment-by-treatment difference.

The three selected Hallmark programs were positively enriched in both comparisons (Fig. 4D). For T-cell co-culture, normalized enrichment scores were 1.44 for apoptosis, 1.88 for TNF signaling via NF-κB and 2.48 for the interferon-γ response, with adjusted P values of 0.0190, 0.000162 and 0.000162, respectively. Corresponding scores for TNF+IFNγ were 1.77, 2.43 and 2.61, with adjusted P values of 0.000539, 0.000198 and 0.000198. The adjustment covered the three selected pathways within each comparison. GO and KEGG over-representation analyses used the corresponding tested-gene backgrounds (Fig. S3A–C). Enrichment of lymphocyte-related terms in co-culture-associated gene lists does not, by itself, establish a tumor-cell-intrinsic response.

Analysis of 11,132 captured CD3-positive cells identified eight transcriptional clusters across the targeted 259-gene panel (Fig. 3D–F; Fig. S2J). Pooled HeLa co-culture cells showed higher mean cytotoxicity, proliferation and TCR-activation module scores and lower naive/memory scores than either control condition. The mean FAS/FASL score was higher than the no-IL-2 control and similar to the IL-2 control (Fig. 3G; Fig. S2I). These comparisons describe the captured samples rather than replicated donor-level treatment effects.

The DepMap analysis retained 1,343 default solid-tumor model profiles. The six experimental models occupied different positions in the expression-based sensing and execution landscapes (Fig. 2E; Fig. S1C,E). Across the available non-melanoma clinical records, nominal Fisher probabilities were 0.000334 for apoptosis, 0.00111 for pyroptosis and 0.639 for necroptosis (Fig. 2D). Dependence among gene tests and the variation visible after omitting individual cohorts limit inferential interpretation of these summary values (Fig. S1D).

For the 25 selected CTRPv2 compound pairs, mean AUC was 13.412 in HeLa and 14.004 in NCI-H460. The mean paired difference was 0.592 AUC units (NCI-H460 minus HeLa; 95% confidence interval 0.079–1.105; two-sided paired t test P = 0.0255; Fig. S1G). This result describes the selected compounds and two model records rather than a general comparison of tumor lineages.
